late-night promises of everyone staring up at it:

> You promised you'd change under a full moon. That was forty-seven full moons ago. I counted.

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. Entry for the
[make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-the-moon-finally-answers-back-781a7ec1).
